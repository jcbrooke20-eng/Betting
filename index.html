How it works: each player’s per-game rebounds, assists, steals and blocks are scaled to projected minutes, then adjusted for the opponent, game pace, team scoring environment, rest and the DFS model’s per-minute outlook. Points come from two methods averaged: a box-score build (field goals, threes, free throws, usage-driven volume, matchup) and the DFS projection. Spread of outcomes comes from the DFS floor and ceiling. Probabilities use count distributions, are blended with DraftKings’ no-vig price, and a bet needs the model to favor that side.
