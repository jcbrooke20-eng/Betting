How it works: per-minute rebound and assist rates come from the season (nudged toward the positional norm in small samples), multiplied by projected minutes and adjusted for the opponent, pace, team scoring, rest and the DFS model’s per-minute outlook. Points average a box-score build and the DFS projection. Count stats use a negative binomial distribution. The result is blended with DraftKings’ no-vig price in log-odds, a bet needs the model to favor that side, bets are ranked by probability edge so long-shot prices don’t get an automatic boost. The big number on each card is the model’s own probability minus the probability implied by DraftKings’ price (vig included), before any blending, so it is the most optimistic view. Bets are filtered and sized from half of the blended EV, a correction for the fact that picking the biggest disagreements overstates them. EV is an estimate, not a promise, and lines post before injury news. If gambling stops being fun, call ConnexOntario at 1-866-531-2600.
