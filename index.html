Track record

How it works: projected minutes are applied to each player’s season rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, adjusted for how many the opponent allows. Points are backed out of the DraftKings projection, so they are the roughest stat. The model’s probability is blended 35/65 with the book’s no-vig price, because a model built from season averages shouldn’t overrule the market. Sizing is quarter-Kelly on a 100-unit bankroll, capped at 2u, halved when projected minutes jump more than 5 over the 10-game average. EV is an estimate, not a guarantee, and a day with no +EV bets should be a day with no bets. If gambling stops being fun, call ConnexOntario at 1-866-531-2600.
