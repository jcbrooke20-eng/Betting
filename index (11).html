How it works: each player’s season rebounds, assists, steals and blocks are scaled to today’s projected minutes, then adjusted for the opponent and the game’s pace. Points are backed out of the DFS projection, so they carry less weight. Probabilities come from count distributions, not a bell curve. The model is blended with DraftKings’ no-vig price (35% model by default), a bet needs the model to favor that side, and sizing is fractional Kelly. EV is an estimate, not a promise.
